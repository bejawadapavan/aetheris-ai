📱 Step 5: Install on Mobile (iOS & Android)

🍎 Apple iPhone (Safari)
  1. Open your Vercel link in Safari
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with arrow up)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Launches standalone without Safari toolbars!
🤖 Google Android (Chrome)
  1. Open your Vercel link in Chrome
  2. Tap the three dots (⋮) menu in top right
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Runs as native app with offline PWA caching!
